1. AI MODEL RELEASES & BREAKTHROUGHS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[OpenAI — GPT-5.4]
OpenAI released GPT-5.4 on March 5, 2026. It is a single unified model that
credibly leads across coding, computer use, reasoning, and knowledge work
simultaneously. The model scored 83.0% on the GDPVal benchmark — at or above
the level of human experts on economically valuable tasks. GPT-5.4 "Thinking"
variant pushes performance further on complex multi-step problems.

[Anthropic — Claude Mythos 5]
Anthropic confirmed Claude Mythos 5 in early April 2026. It is the first AI
model to cross the 10-trillion-parameter threshold, using a Mixture of Experts
(MoE) architecture where only ~800B–1.2T parameters are active per forward pass.
Designed for high-stakes environments: cybersecurity, academic research, and
complex coding. Unlike prior releases, Mythos 5 will NOT be made publicly
available nor offered via standard API.

[Google DeepMind — Gemini 3.1 + TurboQuant]
Google DeepMind's Gemini 3.1 adds real-time voice and image analysis. Separately,
Google's research team unveiled TurboQuant at ICLR 2026 — an algorithm that
slashes AI memory overhead by 6× using PolarQuant vector rotation and Quantized
Johnson-Lindenstrauss (QJL) compression, dramatically lowering inference costs.

[AI Market Landscape — PwC Study]
PwC's 2026 AI Performance Study found that just 20% of companies are capturing
75% of AI's economic gains. Anthropic leads overall model performance rankings,
followed closely by xAI, Google, and OpenAI.
